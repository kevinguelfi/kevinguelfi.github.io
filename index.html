Kevin Guelfi

I am a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at Princeton University. My research agenda focuses on three interrelated dynamics: how political parties adjust their ideological positioning, how voters respond to volatility, and how institutions both constrain and are reshaped by these interactions. I am particularly interested in high-uncertainty contexts such as referendums, close elections, and polarized environments.

Methodologically, I emphasize approaches that enable credible causal inference, including quasi-experiments, field experiments, and latent trait models.

I hold a BA in Political Science from Universidad de Buenos Aires and an MA in Public Policy and Administration from Universidad de San Andrés.
