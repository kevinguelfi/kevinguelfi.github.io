I am a political scientist studying political parties, voter behavior, and political institutions. My research focuses on how parties adjust their ideological positioning under uncertainty and how voters respond to volatility, information, and identity cues, especially in high-stakes contexts such as referendums, crises, and close elections. Methodologically, I work with causal inference and quantitative methods, including quasi-experimental designs, field experiments, and latent trait models.
