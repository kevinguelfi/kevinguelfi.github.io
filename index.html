About me

I am a researcher in political science with interests in political behavior, ideology, public administration, and digital government. My work focuses on the interaction between institutions, political actors, and policy outcomes, using quantitative methods and comparative data.

I am currently applying to PhD programs and working on projects related to voting advice applications, item response theory, and the rule of law.
