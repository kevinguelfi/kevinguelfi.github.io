Kevin Guelfi

I am an MA candidate in Public Policy at the University of Chicago’s Harris School (Class of 2027). My research broadly focuses on democracy and political institutions, with a particular interest in understanding why democratic institutions remain resilient in some contexts while weakening in others. I am especially interested in the role that political parties, leaders, and citizens play in shaping the strength and resilience of democratic institutions, particularly in contexts of political polarization and uncertainty.

Methodologically, I emphasize approaches that enable credible causal inference, including quasi-experiments, field experiments, and latent trait models.

Before joining Harris, I was a Pre-Doctoral Fellow at Princeton University, where I worked on research in political economy and political behavior.

I hold a BA in Political Science from Universidad de Buenos Aires and an MA in Public Policy and Administration from Universidad de San Andrés.
